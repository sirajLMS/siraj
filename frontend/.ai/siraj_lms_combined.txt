## 1. Authentication (Login View)

* **Session Management:** Includes a "Remember me" toggle to persist the user's session.
* **Action:** Form submission triggers authentication validation and redirects to the respective role's dashboard.

### UI & Component Styling

* **Layout:** Clean, minimalist, and perfectly centered vertically and horizontally on a white (`#FFFFFF` or Tailwind `bg-white`) background. No visible card borders or drop shadows; it relies on white space.
* **Branding:** Large, centrally aligned geometric logo above the role selector.
* **Role Tabs:** Inline, horizontally spaced text links (`flex`, `justify-center`, `gap-x`). The active state (Admin) is bolded, while inactive states are lighter/grayed out.
* **Input Fields:** Stacked vertically. They feature a minimal design with a bottom border or very subtle outline, lacking heavy background fills. Each input has a left-aligned icon (a user silhouette for Username, a padlock for Password).
* **Checkbox:** Standard small square checkbox aligned to the left of the "Remember me" text.
* **Primary Button:** A full-width, solid rectangular button with zero or very slight border-radius. The background color is a deep teal/dark green (our primary brand color). The text "SIGNIN" is uppercase, white, and centered.

## 3. "Today Classes" - Metrics & Quick Filters

### Functionality

* **Real-Time Statistics:** The system queries and aggregates the status of all classes scheduled for the current day.
* **Data Categories:** Tracks 12 distinct statuses: Total, Regular, Trial, Advance, Re Scheduled, Suspended, Taken, Pending, Actived, Running, Absent, and Leave.
* **Interactive Filtering:** *Crucial Logic* - These metric cards are not just display elements; they act as quick filters. Clicking a specific card (e.g., "Trial" showing 0) immediately filters the class schedule table below to show only classes matching that status.

### UI & Component Styling

* **Grid Layout:** A responsive, multi-column CSS Grid (likely `grid-cols-2` on smaller screens, up to `grid-cols-4` or `grid-cols-5` on wide monitors) with consistent gaps (`gap-4` or `gap-6`).
* **Card Styling:** * Shape: Rectangular with rounded corners (Tailwind `rounded-xl`).
* Background: Solid white (`bg-white`).
* Shadow: Subtle drop shadow for depth (`shadow-sm` or `shadow-md`).
* Internal Layout: Flexbox container (`flex justify-between items-center`).
* Left side: Large, bold, dark-colored integer representing the count, stacked above the smaller, gray text label (e.g., "Total", "Pending").
* Right side: A distinct icon enclosed in a circular container. The icon and circle border use the primary teal color.
