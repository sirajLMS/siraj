## Siraj LMS - Permissions & Permission Groups (Part 7: 00:00 - 02:02)

### 1. Permission Groups (Roles)

The functionalities introduced in this module govern staff management and schedule generation, which are strictly assigned to administrative roles:

| Permission Group | Description | Assigned Permissions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Academy Admin** | Full authority to manage staff accounts, monitor teacher workloads, and generate new recurring class schedules. | `staff.view_directory`, `schedule.view_weekly`, `schedule.view_workload`, `schedule.create_class` |
| **Teacher** | Restricted to viewing their own weekly schedules; cannot view peer schedules or assign new students to themselves. | `schedule.view_weekly` (Self only), `schedule.view_workload` (Self only) |

---

### 2. Granular Permissions Breakdown

#### Group 1: Staff Directory & Management (`staff`)

Controls access to the global list of educators and high-level workforce metrics.

* **`staff.view_directory`**
* **Description:** Grants access to the global Teachers Directory (`/teachers.php`).
* **Expected Outcome:** Authorizes the rendering of the active/deactivated workforce summary cards and populates the data table with user profiles (`#`, `Image`, `Name`, `Email`, `Zoom`, `Options`, and `Schedule` action links).



#### Group 2: Weekly Schedule Visualization (`schedule`)

Controls the ability to view structured weekly calendar grids and calculate payroll/capacity metrics.

* **`schedule.view_weekly`**
* **Description:** Grants access to view a specific teacher's full weekly calendar grid (Monday - Sunday).
* **Expected Outcome:** Translates database class records into visually plotted, colored time-blocks (displaying Student Name and Duration) against vertical daily time slots. *(Note: Admins can view any teacher ID; Teachers can only query their own ID).*


* **`schedule.view_workload`**
* **Description:** Grants access to view aggregated capacity metrics on the schedule view.
* **Expected Outcome:** Authorizes the calculation and display of the "Weekly hours worked" metric badge at the top of the schedule grid.



#### Group 3: Class Creation & Assignment (`schedule`, `class`)

Controls the ability to create new recurring scheduled sessions and assign students to teachers.

* **`schedule.create_class`**
* **Description:** Grants access to the "Add Class" workflow and its associated database write permissions.
* **Expected Outcome:**
* **Form Access:** Renders the class creation modal with inputs for `Students`, `Time`, `Duration`, and recurring `Days`.
* **Time Override Capability:** Authorizes the UI to render the dynamic "+" icon next to specific days, allowing the user to input custom time overrides that deviate from the primary schedule block (e.g., Mon at 1:00 AM, Wed at 3:00 AM).
* **Execution:** Validates the payload (`Student ID`, `Time String`, `Duration Object`, `Days Array`) and commits the new recurring schedule to the database, subsequently triggering a UI refresh to display the new blocks on the calendar grid.

### 3. Traceability Mapping

> **Note on Security & Workflow Boundaries:**
> * **Separation of Duties (Schedule Creation):** The `schedule.create_class` permission is strictly reserved for Admins. Teachers cannot unilaterally add students to their own schedules or alter the duration/recurrence of their assigned classes, ensuring all billing and workload changes pass through academy management.
> * **Dynamic Constraints:** The ability to inject custom time overrides within the `schedule.create_class` form ensures that Admins do not have to create multiple separate class records for a student with an irregular weekly routine, maintaining a clean relational link between the student, the teacher, and the single "class" entity in the database.
> * **Data Privacy (Workload):** The `schedule.view_workload` permission restricts who can see the "Weekly hours worked" calculation. This ensures that while a teacher can verify their own hours for payroll tracking, they cannot audit the hours or pay estimates of their colleagues.
