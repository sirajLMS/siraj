## Siraj LMS - Permissions & Permission Groups (Part 4: 00:00 - 02:01)

### 1. Permission Groups (Roles)

The new workflows for schedule alteration and absence processing are mapped to the following roles:

| Permission Group | Description | Assigned Permissions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Authority to alter calendar states, process teacher absence requests, and finalize formal schedule modifications. | `session.reschedule`, leverages existing `schedule.filter_advanced` & `dashboard.filter_metrics` |
| **Teacher** | Standard capabilities to manage personal availability by initiating absence workflows. | `session.request_leave` |

---

### 2. Granular Permissions Breakdown

#### Group 1: Leave & Absence Workflows (`session.leave`)

Controls the initiation of formal absence requests, distinct from marking a student absent during a live class.

* **`session.request_leave`**
* **Description:** Grants a user the ability to notify administration of an inability to attend a scheduled class.
* **Expected Outcome:** Updates the class status to "Leave" and increments the corresponding "Leave" metric count on the global Admin Dashboard to trigger an administrative review. *(Note: System routing must be configured to gracefully handle the state change and prevent 404 errors during redirect).*



#### Group 2: Schedule Modification (`session.modification`)

Controls high-level calendar mutations, ensuring only authorized personnel can move classes across different dates and times.

* **`session.reschedule`**
* **Description:** Grants access to the "Reschedule a class" override form within the Class Details view.
* **Expected Outcome:** Authorizes the mutation of the database record's core temporal data (Date and Time). Upon submission, the system forces the class status to `"Re Scheduled"` and automatically injects an audit trail into the Remarks field, documenting both the original and newly assigned dates.



#### Group 3: Forecasting & Verification (`dashboard`, `schedule` - *Extended Application*)

Utilizes previously established permissions to audit future database states.

* **Application of `schedule.filter_advanced` & `dashboard.filter_metrics**`
* **Description:** Admins utilize their existing global read and filter permissions to project the schedule into the future.
* **Expected Outcome:** By submitting a future date string via the date picker and applying the `"Re Scheduled"` metric filter, the system bypasses the "Today" constraint and fetches future calendar records, allowing administrators to visually verify that the `session.reschedule` execution was successful.



---

### 3. Traceability Mapping

> **Note on Security & Workflow Boundaries:**
> * **Separation of Initiation and Execution:** Teachers are granted `session.request_leave` to signal a need for a change, but they **do not** possess `session.reschedule`. This ensures that while a teacher can cancel a class, only an Admin can negotiate and formally move the session on the calendar.
> * **Audit Trail Integrity:** The `session.reschedule` permission guarantees that schedule mutations are not silent. The automated injection of historical dates into the Remarks field ensures that any admin auditing the student's history will understand *when* and *why* the class was moved.
