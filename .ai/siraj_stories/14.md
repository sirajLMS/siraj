## Siraj LMS - Permissions & Permission Groups (Part 15: 00:00 - 01:38)

### 1. Permission Groups (Roles)

The functionalities in this module establish formal, tracked communication pipelines between clients and administration. These are mapped to the following roles:

| Permission Group | Description | Assigned Permissions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Academy Admin** | Authority to read, answer, and destructively manage the centralized support and complaint queue. | `support.admin.view_queue`, `support.admin.answer_complaint`, `support.admin.delete_complaint` |
| **Parent** | Standard capabilities to submit un-tracked feedback or generate tracked support tickets regarding specific students. | `communication.submit_suggestion`, `support.client.submit_complaint`, `support.client.view_history` |
| **System Automated** | Internal background processes responsible for routing communications to external email clients. | `system.dispatch_email_notification` |

---

### 2. Granular Permissions Breakdown

#### Group 1: General Parent Communications (`communication`)

Controls access to open-ended, untracked feedback pipelines.

* **`communication.submit_suggestion`**
* **Description:** Grants the Parent access to the "Suggest" module.
* **Expected Outcome:** Authorizes the submission of open-text feedback. Upon submission, the UI renders an inline success message (`"We Have Recieved Your Message"`), and the system commits the payload without opening a formal tracked ticket on the parent's dashboard.



#### Group 2: Client Support & Ticketing (`support.client`)

Controls the parent's ability to open and track formal, bidirectional support queries.

* **`support.client.submit_complaint`**
* **Description:** Grants access to the formal "Complaints -> Regarding Teacher" workflow.
* **Expected Outcome:** Mounts a scoped form requiring the user to target a specific `Student ID` alongside their complaint text. Submits the record to the database under an `"Un-answered"` status and returns a `"We Will Answer Soon."` confirmation.


* **`support.client.view_history`**
* **Description:** Grants the Parent read-only access to their historical support ledger.
* **Expected Outcome:** Renders the "Complaint Record" view, fetching and displaying all past submissions alongside any resolution text provided by the administration.



#### Group 3: Administrative Support Management (`support.admin`)

Controls the triage, resolution, and deletion of incoming client tickets.

* **`support.admin.view_queue`**
* **Description:** Grants access to the global "Complaints" dashboard.
* **Expected Outcome:** Renders operational metric cards (`Answered`, `Un-answered`) and a chronological feed of all pending tickets, exposing relational data (Parent Name, Student Name, Date, Complaint text).


* **`support.admin.answer_complaint`**
* **Description:** Grants the authority to formally resolve a pending ticket.
* **Expected Outcome:** Allows the Admin to input resolution text and execute the `"Answer & sned to email"` action. The system updates the ticket status to `"Answered"`, logs the response text in the database (making it visible to `support.client.view_history`), and triggers an automated email to the parent.


* **`support.admin.delete_complaint`**
* **Description:** Grants the authority to destructively wipe a ticket from the support system.
* **Expected Outcome:** Exposes the "Delete" action (red trash can). Upon passing the mandatory confirmation prompt (`"Are you sure that you want to delete this Complaint?"`), the system permanently drops the record from the database.



#### Group 4: Automated System Routing (`system`)

Controls the external dispatch of internal platform events.

* **`system.dispatch_email_notification`**
* **Description:** An automated background rule that bridges the LMS with external SMTP/email clients.
* **Expected Outcome:**
* **Trigger 1 (Suggestions):** When `communication.submit_suggestion` is executed, it instantly forwards the payload to the configured academy support inbox so admins do not have to log into the LMS to see general feedback.
* **Trigger 2 (Resolutions):** When `support.admin.answer_complaint` is executed, it dispatches the resolution text directly to the Parent's registered email address.

### 3. Traceability Mapping

> **Note on Security & Workflow Boundaries:**
> * **Tracked vs. Untracked Workflows:** The system enforces a clear distinction between `communication.submit_suggestion` (an untracked, one-way email blast to the admin) and `support.client.submit_complaint` (a formally tracked database entity requiring a structured response).
> * **Relational Ticket Scoping:** The `support.client.submit_complaint` permission forces the parent to link the ticket to a specific `Student ID` via dropdown. This structured data requirement ensures that when Admins view the queue (`support.admin.view_queue`), they immediately know which child and corresponding teacher the issue pertains to, drastically reducing triage time.
> * **Destructive Moderation:** The `support.admin.delete_complaint` permission allows the administration to moderate the queue. This is crucial for removing spam, duplicate submissions, or aggressively inappropriate tickets before they permanently clutter the database.
