- Payments total = converted payouts **+ manual subtract credits** (AC-3.3 single combined figure); FX-excluded sessions contribute neither payout nor credits.
- `aggregate.totalOriginalCurrency` set equal to the converted total (plan §3.3 "same as `sum` in v1").
- Per-row FX conversions run via `Promise.all` (repo-wide `no-await-in-loop`), not sequential.
