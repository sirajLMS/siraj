### Selector notes (locale-independent, role/aria-first)

- MUI **required** fields render the asterisk inside the `<label>`, so the
  accessible name is `"<label> *"` — field selectors use non-anchored
  substring regexes on the translated label; only truly bare labels/buttons
  use anchored `^…$`.
- Radio cards are clicked through the `FormControlLabel` card; the store-driven
  `checked` flip is asserted with a click/retry loop (full-page deep links can
  be clicked before hydration — observed in practice).
- MUI X date pickers are contenteditable section lists (not `<input>`), filled
  via typed digits in the `dd/MM/yyyy` ar-locale order; asserted via the
  rendered year.
- Balance verification prefers the credits list (unique-name search makes the
  row unambiguous), falling back to the pinned `?studentId=` deep-link summary
  chip — parallel suites leak identically-named fixture students
  (e.g. dozens of "SessionIntTest Student" / "s9status Student" rows) into the
  directory; name-only assertions attached to the wrong twin during early runs.
