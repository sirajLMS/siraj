## Summary

Created two new Drizzle schema files for the Monthly Appraisals feature:

1. `backend/db/schema/students/monthly-appraisals.ts` — parent table storing the teacher-authored monthly rubric per `(studentId, teacherId, month)` with nullable 1–5 grade + comment pairs and a required `learningTargets` text field. UNIQUE on `(studentId, teacherId, month)` for idempotent upsert.
