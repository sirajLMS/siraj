## Slot Pattern

The scaffold accepts a `slots` prop with three slots:

| Slot | Type | Common | Desktop | Mobile |
|------|------|--------|---------|--------|
| `LocaleEmoji` | `React.ComponentType<{ emoji: string; sx?: SxProps<Theme> }>` | `LocaleEmoji` | `DesktopLocaleEmoji` | `MobileLocaleEmoji` |
| `CountryOptionRow` | `React.ComponentType<Readonly<CountryOptionRowProps>>` | `CountryOptionRow` | `CountryOptionRowDesktop` | `CountryOptionRowMobile` |
| `optionBoxSx` | `SxProps<Theme>` | `{ px: 2, py: 1 }` | `{ px: 2, py: 1 }` | `{ py: 1.25, minHeight: 48 }` |

All `sx` constants are defined at module scope to comply with `no-object-type-as-default-prop`.
