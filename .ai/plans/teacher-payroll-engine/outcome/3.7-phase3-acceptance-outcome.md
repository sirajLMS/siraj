## Deviation logged (D23)

Task text bullet 2 reads "visible only with `ACCOUNTING_PAYROLL_VIEW` or
`ACCOUNTING_PAYROLL_MANAGE`". The shipped contract is **VIEW-only for both the nav
item and the page guard** (`PAGE_PERMISSIONS.accountingPayrollView` = `[View]`;
`withPageAuth([VIEW])`) — self-consistent (an actor denied by the page never sees the
nav item), identical to the sibling credits precedent, and safe in practice because
bootstrap grants pair VIEW+MANAGE for accounting operators. A MANAGE-only custom
group would see neither the item nor the page. Logged as D23 for the review gate.
