  because generated row `id` is `string | null`; a null id leaves confirm inert.

## Props contract (both dialogs)

`{ open, row, onClose, onDone }` — `row: CarryoverConfirmRow | null`, a `Pick` of the
generated `CarryoverBalanceQuery_carryoverBalance` (`id | teacherName | payable |
teacherCurrency | paymentDate`) plus optional `linkedSessionCount?: number | null`.
Grid rows satisfy the Pick directly.

## i18n

Added `modal.delete.*` (title, linkedSessionsWarning(count), confirm, cancel) and
`modal.unpay.*` (title, warning, note, notePlaceholder, confirm, cancel) to the three
plan-owning locale files (types/en/ar), using the namespace's own per-modal-group
convention (`modal.form.cancel` precedent) and CLDR-shaped AR pluralization with
if/return (no nested ternaries). Toasts reuse existing `success.deleted` /
`success.unpaid`; aria-close + failure fallback come from `Translation.Common`
(`ariaClose`, `operationFailed`). Zero hardcoded user-facing strings.
