     1. `DB_NEON_HTTP_ENABLED !== "false"`
     2. `DB_PROVIDER === "neon"` (case-insensitive)

2. **`backend/db/http-client.ts`:**

   - Updated `clearDbConnectionSingleton()` to clear `HTTP_DB_KEY` and call `clearHttpClientSingleton()`.
   - Re-exported `isNeonHttpEligible`, `getActiveHttpProvider`, `clearHttpClientSingleton`.

4. **Unit Tests:**
