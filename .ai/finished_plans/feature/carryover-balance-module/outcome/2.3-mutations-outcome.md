### Notable adaptation

The shared input-field helper used by the create/update Pothos inputs
(`pothos/teachers/__shared__/carryoverFieldHelpers.ts`) widens `required` flags to
`boolean` at the type level, so `CreateCarryoverInput.payable` / `teacherCurrency`
arrive at the resolver typed `string | null | undefined` /
`CurrencyCode | null | undefined` although the schema enforces them as non-null.
The `createCarryoverBalance` resolver destructure-narrows the two fields and throws
`ValidationError(tErrors.invalidInput)` on absence (schema-level guarantee; the guard
satisfies the canonical `CreateCarryoverInput` type). No Pothos files were modified —
see cross-file note below.

`backend/graphql/mutation/teachers/index.ts` — added `import "./carryover.mutation";`
at the head (side-effect registration per this directory's barrel convention).
Top-level `backend/graphql/mutation/index.ts` already imports `./teachers`; no change.
